## Short description

Coordinate church teams, serving schedules, worship setlists, and events.

## Full description

ServeSync helps church teams stay organized and prepare to serve together.

Keep your ministry schedule and event details in one place. View your assignments, respond to serving invitations, prepare worship setlists, and follow team announcements and conversations.

With ServeSync, participating church teams can:

- View upcoming events and serving assignments.
- Organize worship songs, setlists, and chord charts.
- Communicate through team messages and announcements.
- Coordinate availability, leave, and schedule changes.
- Record attendance where enabled by their church.

An account associated with a participating church is required. The initial release supports our existing church community; availability for additional churches will expand through a pilot.

Developed by BAB Creations.
