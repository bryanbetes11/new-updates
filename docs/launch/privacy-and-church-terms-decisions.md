# ServeSync privacy and church terms decisions

Status: working draft, 2026-09-25. This is a decision and implementation worksheet, not a published Privacy Notice, Terms, or legal opinion. Do not use it as store metadata.

## Verified product data to cover

The live Supabase schema includes:

| Area | Examples found in live tables | Decision needed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Member identity | Name, email, phone, birthday, gender, avatar, church, roles, ministry status and leadership notes | Which fields are necessary and who can view or correct them? |
| Church activity | Events, assignments, availability and leave reasons, attendance, excused reasons, discipline and leader notes | Retention, visibility, correction, and deletion rules; special care for sensitive information |
| Communication | Messages, announcements, attachments, reactions, and read/activity records | Member and admin visibility, moderation, export, deletion, and retention |
| Device and alerts | Push token, installation identifier, notification status and open events | Retention after logout/uninstall and access to activity records |
| Church billing | Plan/trial state, payer and payment reference, receipt URL, review notes | Who can view receipts, financial record retention, and cancellation/refund terms |

The live `announcements`, `avatars`, and `chat-attachments` Supabase Storage buckets are currently marked public. Public bucket status does not by itself prove a leak, but links to private chat attachments must be reviewed before external testing. Bucket-level file-size and MIME restrictions were null in this read-only inspection; inspect application and Storage policy controls before changing limits.

Birthday is optional in registration and onboarding. The current Events calendar uses it for upcoming birthdays and can create a shared birthday announcement. The stored field includes a year; decide whether that precision is necessary for the pilot.

### Attendance use and retention review

The current Attendance Monitoring screen already shows one quarter's assigned events, confirmations/no responses, verified present/late/absent/excused counts, items needing review, offense level, event history, and a CSV export. It does not itself show a comparison across quarters. Its offense levels follow the existing Worship Ministry attendance policy; these are not a new proposed metric.

Clarification of note fields: Attendance Monitoring has a leader-entered `Leader note` when resolving an attendance record (required when selecting Excused). This is distinct from a volunteer's own leave or declined-assignment reason. Team Manage and Discipline also had separate free-text `Leadership Notes` fields. Bryan approved removing those two unused boxes for the pilot on 2026-09-25. Local UI and a guarded migration now remove them; the live database still has the columns until that migration is safely deployed. The migration aborts if substantive content appears before application. Attendance review notes remain.

Working proposal for the pilot: use attendance leader notes only for short, factual corrections such as a failed check-in or an approved schedule change. Do not use the profile/discipline free-text boxes for counseling, health, family, or other intimate details while their visibility and retention remain unverified. This is guidance to review with church leaders, not a product control or a final policy decision.

Potential uses to discuss with church leaders before extending data collection or retention:

1. Compare a member's recent quarters privately to identify changes that warrant a supportive conversation, with excused leave and unreviewed records shown separately.
2. Compare assignment load across team members to avoid over-scheduling the same volunteers and to identify staffing gaps.
3. Show a team-level quarterly summary of coverage, timely confirmations, and unresolved attendance reviews for planning. Avoid public rankings or treating an absence count as a measure of commitment or faith.

Before using attendance for a new purpose, verify the data is accurate enough and explain the purpose to members. Bryan chose to retain identifiable attendance/assignment records for 3 years (12 quarters), then retain only non-identifying team-level summaries while a church is active. Small-team summaries may still identify people, so aggregation needs a disclosure threshold. Named individual history is intended to be visible to the member, church admins, and approved leaders of that member's team only. These are approved policy directions, **not implemented deletion, reporting, or access-control features**.

### Configurable leader oversight — supersedes title-based mapping

- Church admins choose Music, Tech, or both under **Leader attendance access** in Team Manage for a member with a leadership role. A title by itself grants no oversight. A church admin keeps whole-church access without needing group grants.
- **Attendance team** identifies where a volunteer serves. **Leader attendance access** identifies whose attendance a leader may read/manage. These controls are independent.
- No selected oversight groups means own-record access only. Group access is scoped to the church and rechecked against current leadership status. Grants clear when a leader leaves the church or loses their last leadership role.
- Before enforcement, admins must approve member team assignments AND leader oversight assignments. For Bryan's church, select both groups for each director after confirming the actual holders. No automatic assignment or production write was performed.
