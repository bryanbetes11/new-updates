## Full description

ServeSync helps church teams prepare for services and stay connected throughout the week.

Keep your service plans, volunteer assignments, songs, and team conversations together so everyone knows what they are preparing for.

With ServeSync, your church can:

- Plan services and rehearsals with the details your team needs.
- View assignments and manage availability.
- Build and review setlists, then open song charts for practice and services.
- Share announcements and coordinate through team conversations.
- Receive notifications about relevant team activity.

Sign in with your ServeSync account to access your church workspace. Available features depend on your church settings and assigned role. An internet connection is needed for sign-in and shared updates.

## Tester installation note

The previous APK was signed with an Android debug certificate. Moving to the Play-signed app requires a one-time reinstall. Before uninstalling, finish or save any unsent work and verify you can sign back in. Reinstalling removes on-device drafts, cached downloads, settings, and the local login session. It does not itself delete server-side church records. Actual migration and device behavior still need verification.
