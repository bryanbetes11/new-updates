Request deletion of your ServeSync account

You can request account deletion whether or not ServeSync is installed on your phone. You can also ask us to delete specific personal data while keeping your account.

How to request deletion

  1. Email babcreations11@gmail.com from the email address on your ServeSync account. Use the subject “ServeSync account deletion request.”
  2. Tell us your church name and whether you want your whole account deleted or only specific data removed. Do not send your password.
  3. We will verify that the account is yours, coordinate with your church administrator where church-managed records are involved, and explain any information that must be retained.

You can also open App settings → Privacy And Help → Delete account or data in ServeSync to reach this page.

What is deleted or kept

For a verified account deletion request, we remove the sign-in account, profile details, device notification tokens, and personal content tied only to that account. We review messages, attendance, assignments, and other shared church records before removal because deleting them may affect other members or the church's records. Where a legitimate legal, security, or church-record reason requires retention, we will identify the records and reason in our reply. We do not use retained records for advertising.

Active records are handled after verification. Encrypted backups may remain until they rotate; contact us for the current backup schedule. The ServeSync Privacy Notice explains the pilot's retention plan and your other privacy choices.

Need help accessing your account? Email ServeSync support.
