Private Pilot Terms

Version 25 September 2026 · free invited-church pilot

ServeSync is operated by Bryan Ashley Lopez Betes as an individual in the Philippines. These terms apply to a church administrator who accepts a private pilot invitation and to adults the church invites into its workspace. Questions: babcreations11@gmail.com.

The pilot is free, invitation-only, and initially available for 30 days from church activation. We may agree with a church to extend it. There is no automatic paid conversion or charge; paid use would require separate terms and an affirmative decision.

Church responsibilities

The church chooses who to invite, the roles it grants, and the information it records. Its administrator must invite only adults to the outside-church pilot, keep roles current, explain ministry data uses to members, address member requests first, and obtain any permissions required for its own processing. Do not enter counseling, medical, family, or other highly sensitive notes into general chat or attendance fields. The church must not upload material it lacks the right to share.

Member responsibilities

Use your own account, keep credentials private, and respect other members' information. Send only content needed for church coordination. Do not harass others, impersonate someone, try to access another church's data, or use automated requests to overload the service. Report a safety or security concern to your church administrator and to ServeSync when platform help is needed.

Service and support

This is a test version. Features may change, and occasional interruption is possible. We will work to correct material problems and protect stored information, but cannot promise uninterrupted availability. Send bug reports and support requests to babcreations11@gmail.com. We may suspend access to protect users or the service from misuse, and will explain the action when practical.

Information, export, and exit

The Privacy Notice explains the information handled by ServeSync. A church may end the pilot by emailing us. We will offer its administrator an export of operational church records, excluding private one-to-one chats and personal notes, and plan to remove its active workspace within 30 days after exit. Individual access, correction, and removal requests start with the church administrator, with ServeSync available as a fallback. Legal obligations or backup rotation can affect complete deletion; we will explain any exception in response to a request.

Ownership and changes

The church and its members keep rights in content they provide. They allow ServeSync to host and transmit it solely to operate and support the pilot. ServeSync keeps rights in its software and design. If these pilot terms materially change, we will present the new version before continued pilot use.
