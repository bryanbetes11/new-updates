Privacy Notice

Private pilot · effective 25 September 2026

ServeSync helps churches plan services and coordinate volunteers. Bryan Ashley Lopez Betes operates ServeSync as an individual in the Philippines. For privacy or security questions, email babcreations11@gmail.com.

A church chooses its members, roles, schedules, and ministry records. Please contact your church administrator first to access, correct, or remove church-managed information. You may contact ServeSync directly if the administrator is unavailable or your request concerns the platform itself.

Information we handle

We store account details such as name, email, optional birthday, profile photo, church and role; schedules, assignments, availability, attendance and reasons; announcements, messages and attached files; notification settings and device tokens; and security and app-activity records needed to run the service. Church administrators can see operational records for their church. A member can see their own attendance. Approved leaders can see attendance only for the Music or Tech group assigned by their administrator. Chat participants can see their conversations; a church-wide export does not include private one-to-one chats.

Why and how it is used

We use these details to authenticate accounts, connect people to the correct church, deliver schedules and updates, maintain attendance and ministry records, send requested notifications, support users, and protect the service. Churches are responsible for explaining their own ministry uses and choosing a lawful basis for data they ask members to provide. We do not sell member data or use it for third-party advertising.

Who receives information

Other authorized members and leaders in your church may see information according to their roles. Supabase hosts authentication, database, files, and server functions; Vercel serves the website; Google Firebase Cloud Messaging handles Android push notifications. These providers may process information outside the Philippines under their service arrangements. A notification preview may appear on a device lock screen if enabled. External sites opened from ServeSync have their own practices.

Photos and files

Chat attachments and announcement images use access checks and short-lived links. A person who already received or downloaded a file may keep a copy. Profile photos currently use shareable image links: someone with the exact link may be able to view the photo without signing in. Profile photos are optional; do not upload a sensitive image.

Retention and removal

During the pilot, we keep records while the church uses ServeSync and respond to specific access or deletion requests. Our retention plan is to remove routine messages and chat attachments after 12 months, and identifiable attendance and assignment records after 3 years, subject to justified legal exceptions. The automatic cleanup is still being prepared; contact us to request earlier removal. When a church leaves, we offer its administrator an operational-data export and plan to remove its active workspace within 30 days. Encrypted backups may remain for a limited recovery period before rotation; contact us for the current schedule. Shared records may need review before deleting one person's account so another member's legitimate records are not erased without notice.

Account and data deletion

To request deletion of your ServeSync account and associated data, or to ask us to remove specific data while keeping your account, follow the steps on our account deletion page. You can make a request from the website even after uninstalling the app.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct, export, object to processing, or request removal of your information. Start with your church administrator for church-managed records, or email us directly. We will verify the request and explain any lawful retention or technical limits. You can disable push notifications in the app or device settings. Signing out stops future account access on that device but does not delete server records.

Children and the pilot

New churches in the private pilot may invite adults only. Existing churches may have younger volunteers; their administrators remain responsible for informing families and managing participation. Parents or guardians can contact the church administrator or ServeSync about a child's information. The pilot age rule does not mean all existing users are adults.

Changes and contact

We may revise this notice as the pilot changes. We will show material changes before asking pilot churches to continue under new terms. For a privacy concern, suspected exposure, or account request, email babcreations11@gmail.com.
